🚀 System Gateway & Mission Briefing

OracleAI is an explainable, multi-horizon disaster intelligence platform combining 76 years of climate learning (Gridded IMD 1951–2021 + Copernicus ERA5 2022–2026) with real-time numerical weather forecast inputs to generate district-level flood and heatwave risk predictions for 24, 48, and 72-hour horizons across India.
